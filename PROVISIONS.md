# Provisions Matrix

The method lives on Street Cypher and Private School Cypher. See `CYPHERS.md`.
K12 is a later export. It does not author PROTOCOL.

## readarap.com = Street Cypher (community floor)
Audience: open cipher. `audience = street`.
Rules: community code + terms of use + copyright on source tracks + age-gate.
Default 18+. 13–17 only with a guardian as account owner. No under-13 accounts.
Do not claim compulsory-attendance exemption. Street is not school.
Keep the raw energy. Still no illegal content. No ripped MP3 library.
No FERPA packet, no SIS, no standards tags in the player.

## readarapacademy.com = Private School Cypher (school floor)
Audience: enrolled pupils + correspondence. `audience = academy`.
Legal hook: California Private School Affidavit, EC 33190 and 48222.
File yearly at https://www3.cde.ca.gov/psa (open Aug 1-Jun 30; statutory Oct 1-15).
Must offer the several branches of study, keep pupil records, course of study, and a custodian of records.
Course of study for the affidavit = PROTOCOL. Do not put standards language in the player.
PSA is registration, not accreditation, not CDE endorsement, not ADA funding.
Virtual / correspondence delivery is allowed if the school actually instructs.
Teacher-before-student is on. Coach models the track.
Do not sell this skin as a district Classroom or School license.

## Prison / inmate offer (Private School Cypher only)
Paper-first correspondence packets. Facility education-principal approval.
No unapproved internet, no live mics unless the yard authorizes it.
Do not claim we are an official CDCR vendor until a contract exists.
Offer: Name Flo sheet + Alphabet Buss Down packet + lyric-swap worksheets mailed in and graded out.
Never under readarap.com or readarap.org.

## readarap.org = K12 export (parked)
Audience: students, families, districts. `audience = k12`.
Vendor provisions — FERPA, COPPA under 13, content ratings, parental consent, mandated reporter, accessibility, clean classroom lyrics, standards-aligned lesson language — apply **only here**.
No claiming public-school status unless a real LEA partnership exists.
If selling into schools: vendor packet, insurance, data processing addendum.
These rules must not leak into the cipher player on .com or .academy.
