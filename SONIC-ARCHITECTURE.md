# Sonic architecture of the genre

Readarap is an inverted contrafact.

Jazz contrafact: new tune, old chords.
Readarap: new subject text, old pocket.

The loved song donates time, stress, rests, and rhyme terminals.
The hated subject donates meaning.
The lead instrument is timed speech, not a new sung hook.

If you hear a bounce written for school, you are in the rejected genre.
If you hear a pocket you already know carrying words you used to skip, you are in Readarap.

Do not treat this file as a transcription of any hit. Architecture is the grid. The loved recording stays in the learner's other tab, or never plays.

## Layer 0 — Time grid

A bar is four beats unless a track says otherwise. A take is eight or sixteen bars. No grid, no take.

tracks.bpm is the clock.

- Name Flo / paper / young: 70–90 BPM (example bed 80). Speech-intelligible.
- Cipher beds we ship: 80–110. Boom-bap pocket. Snare on 2 and 4.
- Half-time / trap clock: 140–160 felt at 70–80. Same syllable math.

Kick on 1. Clap is the syllable pad. Silence is architecture. School-rap fills every eighth. Readarap keeps the loved song's rests.

## Layer 1 — Name Flo as tuning

The first instrument is the learner's name.
Stress feet: iamb da-DUM, trochee DUM-da, amphibrach da-DUM-da, dactyl DUM-da-da.
Clap = onset of a syllable. Cadence window ~±12 percent of tracks.bpm.
A hated-subject word that will not sit on that map gets rewritten. Do not stuff.

## Mix buses (the four pillars)

- Syllable Lock 0.35 — quantize. Hits match syllable_target.
- Cadence Key 0.30 — pocket. Where hits sit, including rests.
- Rhyme Graph 0.25 — terminals. End-family of the scheme, not the loved word.
- Fact Check 0.10 — payload. Hated subject is true. One fact per four bars.

Pass: weighted >= 70 AND syllable >= 60.

## 50 / 75 / 100 as arrangement

- 50 Baby — record still in the room. Loved scaffolding holds the pocket.
- 75 Little — record is a ghost. Recognition rides scheme + pocket + stress.
- 100 Big — subject owns the pocket. Zero loved words. If they can sing the original from our sheet, we shipped the wrong object.

## Take cut

Count-in (2 bars) → original bed or click → swap in 4-bar rooms → rest / lock → Baby Little Big stamp.

Same cut on Street and Academy. If Academy sounds brighter, slower, and more helpful, the genre leaked.

## Map object (generic)

4/4, 88 BPM, 8 bars, syllable_target 8-8-9-8, rhyme_family -ay/-ay/-own/-own, subject = fractions.
That is a map. It is not a recording we stole.
