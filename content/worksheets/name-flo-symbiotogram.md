# Name Flo — Symbiotogram Worksheet (Pencil Stage)

**Purpose:** Turn your name into a character. The flipped word is the secret identity. The block-letter drawing is the public face.

**Rule:** Eyes, nose, two arms, two legs. Placement is the art.

---

## Step 1 — Write Your Name
Write your full name (or first name) in **all capital letters** on the line below.

_______________________________________________

## Step 2 — Flip It 180°
Turn the paper upside down. Look at the last letter — it becomes the first letter of a new word.

What word do you see?

## Step 3 — Make It a Real Word
If it doesn't read as a word yet, adjust the strokes. Each letter must serve **two** letters (one upright, one flipped).

Try these easy pairs first:
- MOM ↔ WOW
- DEAL ↔ LEAP
- TRUE ↔ FALSE
- NOON ↔ NOON (same word — good starter)

## Step 5 — The Public Face
Now write your **original name** again in block letters. Add eyes, a nose, two arms, two legs. Make it a person, an animal, a monster — whatever the letters suggest.
