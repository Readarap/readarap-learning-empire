# The genre

**Readarap turns music into a new genre using the songs we love and the subjects we hate.**

That sentence is the art. It does not replace the method.

- **Method:** lock syllables to a beat. Keep cadence, rhyme scheme, and syllable count. Swap the lyric.
- **Genre:** what that method produces when a pocket you already love carries a subject you refused to open.

The rejected genre was original school-rap — clean kids songs written to sound educational. That is K12 packaging. It is not Readarap.

The claimed genre is the collision: their record, the chapter they hated, the same bar count.

Every karaoke take has two parents: a loved song (cadence / rhyme / count) and a hated subject (the lyric in the blanks).

Name Flo and Alphabet Buss Down are the on-ramp. The genre lives at 50 / 75 / 100.

Fact Check (0.10) is how the hated subject stays true. It is not a quiz format.
