# Inmate Correspondence Track (Academy only)

Lives under readarapacademy.com. Never under readarap.org (K12) and never under readarap.com (Street Cypher).

## Offer
Paper-first adult literacy correspondence using the shared protocol:
1. Name Flo worksheet — learner name broken into syllable beats
2. Alphabet Buss Down packet — 26 letters, one page each, no internet
3. Freestyle Karaoke lyric-swap sheets at 50 / 75 / 100 percent
4. Written feedback + RapCoin / Baby-Little-Big stamp on the return

No live mic. No in-cell app login unless the facility education department already approved a device program.

## Why paper-first
Most yards restrict recording devices, unapproved internet, and kits that look like tools. Correspondence packets a sponsor or education staff can screen are the realistic door.

## Rules
- Adult learners only. Separate roster from K12. Separate data store.
- Facility education principal must approve content and delivery before mail-in.
- Family, chaplain, or education staff can sponsor postage.
- Do not claim CDCR vendor status until a vendor packet is accepted.
- Do not promise a California diploma from a Private School Affidavit filing alone.
- Offer: literacy progress, Readarap Academy certificate of completion, lyric-swap portfolio.
- Facility-safe content only: no maps, no coded language, no contraband, no gang signs, no explicit tracks.
- Same scoring engine: Syllable 0.35 / Rhyme 0.25 / Cadence 0.30 / Fact 0.10.

## One-month packet
- Cover letter to Education Department
- Name Flo sheet + worked example + blank for the learner
- 8 Alphabet Buss Down pages (letter-of-the-week rotation)
- One clean educational karaoke sheet with 50 / 75 / 100 swap columns
- Return instructions for the sponsor
- Progress card: Baby / Little / Big stamp line

## Honest limit
This is adult literacy correspondence attached to a private-school filing. It is a second-chance reading door. It is not a prison school contract until a facility signs one.
